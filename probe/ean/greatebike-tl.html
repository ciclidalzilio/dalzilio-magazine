The Versatile Leader Among Electric SUV Bikes

The Amflow TL Carbon represents a revolutionary combination of a lightweight full-carbon frame, the extreme power of the Avinox motor, and top-of-the-line FOX suspension. This fully equipped electric SUV bike offers uncompromising technology and a massive load capacity at a price that ’s unmatched on the market.

FOX Suspension: Comfort on Any Terrain

The full-suspension setup combines a 120mm FOX AWL Sport front fork with a 105mm FOX FLOAT Rhythm rear shock. This system is precisely tuned for SUV loads, so it smoothly handles everything from city cobblestones to challenging forest trails.

Fully equipped SUV without any additional investment

The bike comes fully equipped with aluminum fenders, a kickstand, a basket, and integrated Supernova LED lights. It also includes a premium rear rack with the MIK HD system, certified for mounting a child seat.

Power on the Go: Removable 800Wh Battery

Thanks to its sophisticated locking mechanism, the Avinox RS800’s built-in 800Wh battery can be easily removed with one hand for convenient charging at home or in the office. For extreme range, the system is also compatible with an external 480Wh Range Extender.

Smartphone Connectivity

The Avinox Ride app allows for complete bike tuning, GPS location tracking, and offline navigation on the bike’s display. The system is integrated with Apple’s Find My network and offers advanced anti-theft security, including an alarm and remote monitoring of your bike’s technical condition.

Amflow TL Carbon: A Revolutionary, No-Compromise SUV E-bike

The Amflow TL Carbon represents the absolute pinnacle in the segment of modern, fully equipped SUV e-bikes, blurring the lines between sports equipment and a practical mode of transportation. It is designed to effortlessly handle the morning commute to work, a fully equipped weekend trip, and challenging mountain terrain off paved roads. It shatters established standards in its class—offering a premium carbon frame and top-of-the-line components at a price at which competitors often offer only heavier aluminum models with half the features.

At the heart of this exceptional machine is the ultra-light Amflow TL Carbon SUV Frame, an all-carbon frame with optimized geometry for all-day comfort, weighing just 2,875 g. Despite its rugged SUV-style components, the bike’s total weight comes in at an impressive 22.6 kg. Combined with a well-engineered low center of gravity, this ensures brilliant handling on singletrack and excellent stability on the road. The frame is also rated for an extreme total load of up to 200 kg, so it can easily carry both the rider and heavy luggage on expedition trips.

At the heart of the drivetrain is the revolutionary Avinox M2 motor, developed in collaboration with a technology giant in the field of drones and stabilization. This motor delivers a nominal torque of 110 Nm and, in Boost mode, can generate a massive 125 Nm with a maximum power output of 1100 W, allowing you to tackle even the steepest climbs with absolute ease. The system is powered by a massive integrated Avinox RS800 battery with a capacity of 800 Wh, which provides exceptional range even under heavy loads and can be easily removed from the frame.

The entire ecosystem is controlled via the sleek 2" Avinox DPC100 OLED touchscreen display integrated into the top tube and wireless controls on the handlebars. It also features a USB-C charging port for peripherals and advanced connectivity with an integrated 4G module and GPS. The icing on the cake is native support for Apple Find My, which ensures uncompromising security and real-time tracking of the bike’s location.

Key Features and Technical Specifications:

  • Powerful DJI Avinox M2 drive: Rated torque of 110 Nm (up to 125 Nm in Boost mode) and peak power of 1100 W with Smart Assist mode.
  • Lightweight carbon frame: A premium carbon fiber frame weighing just 2.875 kg with internal cable routing and an extreme total load capacity of up to 200 kg.
  • Electronic automatic shifting: 12-speed wireless TRP E.A.S.I. A12 electronic derailleur with Auto Shift technology and manual control option.
  • Uncompromising braking power: Magura MT5 (or TRP Slate EVO)four-piston hydraulic brakes with massive 203mm rotors (2.3 mm thick) for safely stopping a fully loaded bike.
  • Durable wheelset: 27.5" Amflow SUV Alloy rims (35 mm inner width) fitted with top-of-the-line 2.6" wide Maxxis Rekon tires for perfect grip and comfort on any surface. 29" wheels can also be used by removing the fenders.
  • Smart features and 4G connectivity: Integrated 2" OLED touchscreen, Bluetooth, Wi-Fi, LTE mobile network with GPS tracking, and full integration into the Apple Find My ecosystem.
  • Unbeatable value: A unique combination of full FOX suspension, a carbon frame, the revolutionary Avinox motor, and a full suite of SUV-style features—all at a price unmatched on the market today.

This e-bike model supports connection to Apple's Find My service. This innovative feature allows users to track the location of their e-bike very accurately using the Find My app on their Apple devices. Apple's Find My network allows you to securely track your e-bike through a wide range of Apple devices. The bike's location is anonymised and encrypted so only the owner has access to it. This ensures a high level of security. The tracker is cleverly integrated into the motor housing of the e-bike and can be supplied with power through the normal charging process. It also has its own rechargeable battery that allows it to operate for up to a year without recharging. Carbon fibre

E-bikes that use carbon fiber are a common choice among amateur and professional cyclists. Previously, it was mainly road e-bikes, but today you can enjoy the benefits of carbon on almost any type of e-bike. It remains an ideal choice among lovers of fast driving and off-road driving. The main advantage of e-bikes with a carbon frame is exceptional shock and vibration damping, they resist corrosion and wear very well and, compared to other materials, they excel in their low weight.

Avinox M2

The perfect balance of power and efficiency, with 110 Nm of torque (125 Nm with boost), a peak power output of 1100 W, and a lightweight design (2.65 kg). The second generation with helical gears and Dual Gear Engagement technology ensures quiet operation without knocking during descents. New bearings minimize resistance for a natural riding feel, while improved cooling ensures stability even in the heat. Integrated sensors enable adaptive power control based on cadence and riding conditions, making it the ideal, smooth workhorse for modern trail riding.

Avinox RS800 Battery

The Avinox RS800 battery, with a high capacity of 800 Wh, combines maximum power with the practicality of a removable system. Weighing 4.0 kg and featuring IP56 protection, it can be easily removed from the frame thanks to a locking mechanism and charged anywhere away from the bike. When paired with the M2S motor, it delivers an impressive continuous power output of 1300 W and can reach a peak of 1500 W in Boost mode. Intelligent health management ensures 80% capacity retention after 500 cycles, and GaN technology enables ultra-fast charging.

More information

Avinox DPC100 Display

A smart 2" OLED touchscreen integrated into the frame with a high resolution of 326 ppi and 800 nits of brightness, which automatically adjusts to the surroundings. It is equipped with GPS sensors, a gyroscope, a barometer, and support for Bluetooth 5.1 and ANT+. The DPC100 model stands out with support for Apple Find My for easy bike tracking and allows you to adjust virtually all system functions directly on the screen without needing an app. It records your route, performance, and cadence in detail. Controls are complemented by wireless handlebar controls (right controller available only on select models) and a built-in 10W USB-C port for charging accessories while riding.

More information

Extended 5-year warranty on Amflow frames

Amflow provides the original owner with a 5-year warranty on the carbon frame (excluding paint and graphics). A major benefit is the option to transfer the warranty to a second owner, for whom the warranty is valid for 2 years from the date of the original purchase of the e-bike. The stem, handlebars, wheels, paint, and graphics are covered by a 2-year warranty, while minor accessories are covered by a 1-year warranty.

The drive system (motor, battery, display, controls, and charger) is covered by a 2-year warranty, with the battery limited to 2 years or 500 charge cycles. Claims for other third-party components (brakes, shifting, suspension) are handled directly according to the terms of their respective manufacturers.

In addition, the first owner receives a one-time discount on a new carbon frame or wheels in the event of an accident (crash replacement). The warranty does not cover normal wear and tear on consumable parts, such as tires, bearings, chains, or brake pads.
