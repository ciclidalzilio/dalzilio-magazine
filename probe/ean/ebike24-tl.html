Description

The Amflow TL Carbon is a performance‑focused SUV e‑bike with all‑terrain DNA, built on an advanced carbon frame and specifically engineered for maximum efficiency, range and versatility. The ultra‑light monocoque carbon frame (approx. 2.9 kg) combines high stiffness with low weight and meets demanding load and durability standards for real‑world riding conditions.

At its core is the Avinox M2 drive unit, delivering peak output of up to 1100 W and a maximum torque of 125 Nm. The system features five finely tuned assistance modes, as well as intelligent traction control that dynamically adapts power delivery to the terrain and riding conditions. The modular energy system includes a removable high‑performance battery (800 Wh) with an optional range extender, enabling ranges of up to 270 km.

Features of the Amflow TL Carbon – 2027

  • 27.5‑inch carbon frame (approx. 2.9 kg)
  • Avinox M2 drive system – 125 Nm torque
  • Avinox DPC100 2‑inch OLED display
  • Avinox RS800 battery (removable)
  • Integrated smart control system with navigation, app connectivity and sensors
  • Intelligent traction control and 5 assistance modes (Auto, Eco, Trail, Turbo, Boost)
  • FOX full suspension: 120 mm front / 105 mm rear for high riding stability
  • Maximum system weight: approx. 200 kg (incl. rider and luggage)
  • Size adjustable via stem and dropper post
  • Modular expandability (MIK system, trailer use, child seat compatibility)
  • Maxxis Crossmark II SUV tyres
